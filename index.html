The Art of Creation

01

Conceptualization

First, we conceptualize your jewelry. No matter what you want, together, we shape your ideas.

02

3D CAD Design

With precision, we give volume and dimensions to the previous concept, digitizing your idea.

03

Wax Printing

Once we have the final 3D design, we print it in special wax so we can then transfer it to the metal of your choice.

04

Casting

Using the traditional lost-wax method to seamlessly transform the fragile prototype into solid, precious metal.

05

Final Finishing

Meticulous hand polishing, precise gem setting, and the ultimate quality review of your bespoke masterpiece.

Meet the Designer

When a client struggles to visualize what they want, I help them bring their ideas to life through the digital previsualization and rendering process.

This is how ideas begin to materialize.

I love being part of the entire process, from the initial concept on a screen to being able to hold it in my own hands.

The digital and the tangible are two worlds i navigate with ease.

My work experience to date has been very diverse, which has taught me various techniques and perspectives to see the world from different angles.

Without a doubt, what I enjoy most professionally is working in the field of FASHION.

People incorporate objects into their daily routine that hold meaning, a meaning so profound that they feel the need to share it with the world.

When someone incorporates a design we created together into their everyday look, I experience that feeling of triumph that motivates me every day to keep designing.
